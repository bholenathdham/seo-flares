FAQ

Ecommerce questions, answered clearly

Do you build ecommerce websites in Dubai?

Yes. SEO Flares can work on ecommerce design and development for Dubai businesses, with the scope shaped around the catalogue, customer journey and technical requirements.

Can you build a store from an existing design?

Yes. An existing approved design can be translated into a responsive ecommerce experience, provided the design and required functionality are clearly defined.

Can you redesign an existing ecommerce website?

Yes. A redesign can address visual presentation, product hierarchy, navigation, responsive behaviour and the underlying structure while keeping the useful parts of the existing store in view.

Do you design for mobile ecommerce?

Yes. Mobile layouts, touch interactions, product presentation, navigation and purchase actions are considered as part of the storefront rather than treated as an afterthought.

Can ecommerce development support SEO?

Yes. Store architecture, category structure, product pages, internal links, clean URLs and useful content can provide a stronger technical foundation for future SEO work. No particular ranking outcome is guaranteed.

Can you work with an existing ecommerce platform?

That depends on the platform, existing setup and required functionality. The first step is to understand what is already working and what needs to change before deciding on the implementation approach.

Can the store be maintained after launch?

The build can be structured with future catalogue and content changes in mind. Ongoing maintenance can be discussed according to the store's platform and requirements.
