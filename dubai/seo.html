BEYOND THE KEYWORD

SEO is not one task
It is a connected system

A website can have good-looking pages and still make it difficult for search engines to understand what matters. It can also rank for relevant searches and still fail to communicate once a visitor arrives.

A stronger SEO strategy looks at the complete path — from the search query and technical foundation to the page itself and the next action a visitor may take.

02 · SEARCH INTENT

Start with what
the search means

Keyword research becomes more useful when it is connected to the reason behind a search.

Someone searching for a service may be ready to compare options. Someone asking a question may need information first. A local search may depend heavily on location and proximity.

We use those differences to help determine which pages should target which search themes and what those pages actually need to communicate.

SEO FAQ · DUBAI

Practical questions
Straight answers

What SEO services do you provide in Dubai? +

Our SEO work can include technical SEO, keyword and search-intent research, on-page optimisation, internal linking, content structure and local SEO depending on the needs of the website and business.

Do you provide local SEO for businesses in Dubai? +

Yes. Where local search is relevant, we can work on location structure, local relevance, business information and other appropriate local SEO foundations.

Is SEO mainly about keywords? +

No. Keywords are one part of the process. Technical accessibility, search intent, content relevance, internal linking, page structure and website experience all contribute to a useful SEO strategy.

Can SEO be included when building a new website? +

Yes. Planning SEO alongside the website structure can help information architecture, page hierarchy, content and technical foundations work together from the beginning.

Can you improve SEO on an existing website? +

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed across technical, structural, content and on-page areas to identify practical opportunities for improvement.

How long does SEO take? +

SEO does not have one fixed timeline. The work and pace depend on the website, market, competition, technical condition, content and the opportunities available.
