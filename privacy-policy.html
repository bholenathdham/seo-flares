About SEO Flares

SEO Flares is a digital services business providing web design, website development, search engine optimisation and digital strategy services.

SEO Flares works with businesses and organisations in different locations and provides digital services through its website and online communications.

In the course of operating our website and providing our services, we may receive or process information relating to individuals who visit our website, communicate with us, request information, or become clients.

This Privacy Policy explains the principles and practices we apply to that information.

02

Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the SEO Flares website, including information submitted through our contact and project enquiry forms.

It also applies to personal information that you may provide when communicating with us about our services, projects, proposals, partnerships or general business enquiries.

This policy does not automatically apply to websites, platforms or services operated by third parties that may be linked from our website. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

Where a separate agreement, proposal, statement of work or other contractual document contains specific provisions concerning personal data, those provisions may apply alongside this Privacy Policy.

03

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with SEO Flares.

Information you provide directly

When you submit a project enquiry or communicate with us, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business or organisation name
  • Your website address
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your approximate project budget or range
  • Your project requirements
  • Your goals, challenges and priorities
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your message

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting environment or technical infrastructure may process limited technical information required to deliver and secure the website.

Depending on the hosting and infrastructure used at a particular time, this may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring page, requested pages, approximate access time and technical error information.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our general contact form.

04

How information is collected

Personal information may be collected when you:

  • Submit the contact form on our website
  • Send us an email
  • Request information about our services
  • Discuss a potential project with us
  • Become a client or work with us on a project
  • Communicate with us during an existing project
  • Interact with our website or its technical infrastructure

We aim to collect information fairly and transparently and, where required, provide appropriate notice about the purpose for which information is collected.

05

How we use personal information

We use personal information only for purposes that are relevant to operating our business, responding to communications, delivering services and managing our relationship with clients and prospective clients.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Understanding your project requirements
  • Preparing project proposals or estimates
  • Discussing potential website, development, SEO or strategy work
  • Delivering agreed services
  • Managing client communications
  • Maintaining appropriate business records
  • Protecting our website, systems and business from misuse or security threats
  • Complying with applicable legal or regulatory requirements

We do not intend to use personal information collected through our enquiry form for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis for doing so.

06

Contact enquiries and project information

Our contact form is designed to allow potential clients to explain what they are trying to build, improve or achieve.

Information you submit may therefore contain business information, project objectives, website details, technical requirements, timelines, budgets or descriptions of current problems.

We use this information to understand the enquiry, determine whether our services are appropriate, communicate with you and, where applicable, prepare a proposal or project plan.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, health information or other highly sensitive information through the general contact form.

07

Contact form processing through Web3Forms

SEO Flares uses Web3Forms as a third-party service to process submissions made through our website contact form.

When you submit the form, the information entered into the form is transmitted to Web3Forms so that the enquiry can be processed and delivered to us.

Information submitted through the form may include your name, email address, business or website, selected service, project range and message.

Web3Forms is a separate service provider and may process information according to its own terms, privacy practices and technical infrastructure. You should review the provider's current documentation and privacy information for further details.

We select third-party providers with consideration for the nature of the information being processed and the services they provide.

08

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that may be stored on a device when a website is visited.

SEO Flares may use essential technical mechanisms that are necessary for website operation, security, performance or functionality.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, marketing pixels or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will review the applicable notification and consent requirements and update this Privacy Policy where appropriate.

Your browser may also provide controls for managing cookies and similar technologies. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

09

Third-party services

Operating a modern digital business may require the use of selected third-party technology providers.

Depending on the services currently used by SEO Flares, these providers may support areas such as:

  • Website hosting
  • Domain and DNS infrastructure
  • Contact form processing
  • Email communication
  • Website security
  • Website performance and technical infrastructure

Third-party providers may process information on our behalf or independently according to their own contractual terms and privacy policies.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that we do not operate.

10

Disclosure of personal information

SEO Flares does not sell personal information.

We may disclose or provide access to personal information where reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes, service delivery, technical operation, legal compliance or protection of our rights.

This may include disclosure to:

  • Service providers supporting our website or business operations
  • Professional advisers where appropriate
  • Contractors or project personnel who need information to perform agreed services
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law

We seek to limit access to personal information to people and providers who reasonably need it for the relevant purpose.

11

International and cross-border data transfers

SEO Flares may work with clients, contractors, technology providers or infrastructure located in different countries.

As a result, personal information may be processed or stored outside the country in which you are located.

Where personal information is transferred across borders, we aim to use appropriate contractual, organisational or technical safeguards and to comply with applicable requirements concerning international transfers.

The specific safeguards that apply may depend on the country involved, the type of information, the service provider and the applicable privacy law.

12

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

Security measures may include appropriate access controls, secure transmission mechanisms, account protections, limited access to information and reasonable measures designed to protect the systems used to operate our website and business.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If we become aware of a personal data incident that requires notification under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain and respond to the incident and make notifications where legally required.

13

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to manage a business relationship, to maintain appropriate records, or where retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

The appropriate retention period depends on the nature of the information and the reason it was collected.

For example, a general project enquiry may not need to be retained for the same period as information relating to an active client relationship or contractual business record.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

14

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the laws that apply to the processing of your personal information, you may have rights concerning information held about you.

Depending on the applicable legal framework, these may include rights relating to:

  • Access to personal information
  • Information about how personal information has been used or disclosed where applicable
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent
  • Deletion or cessation of processing where a legally applicable right exists
  • Other rights provided under applicable data-protection legislation

Requests should be sent to hello@seoflares.info .

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request. This is intended to help protect personal information from unauthorised disclosure.

Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions, reasonable administrative requirements or other limitations permitted by applicable law.

15

Children's privacy

Our services are intended primarily for businesses, organisations and professional users. The website is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children through our general enquiry form.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us and there is a legal reason for that information to be removed, please contact us at hello@seoflares.info .

16

Eligibility and Regional Restrictions

SEO Flares provides its website, services, and related communications primarily to users and businesses located outside the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA).

Our services are not intended for users located in the EU or EEA. SEO Flares does not intentionally market, offer, or provide its services to individuals or businesses located in these regions and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals located in the EU or EEA through this website.

Our website may be technically accessible from locations around the world; however, accessibility of the website does not mean that SEO Flares is intentionally offering or marketing its services to users in the EU or EEA.

If you are located in the EU or EEA, please do not submit personal information through this website or request our services.

These regional restrictions do not limit or exclude any rights or obligations that may apply to SEO Flares under applicable laws and regulations. Where applicable law imposes obligations on SEO Flares notwithstanding these restrictions, SEO Flares will comply with its applicable legal requirements.
