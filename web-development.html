The code is part of the experience

That means thinking beyond the appearance of a single screen and considering how the whole site behaves across devices, pages and real content.

SITE ARCHITECTURE

A website is a system of connected pages

Good development starts with understanding how the pages relate to each other. Navigation, shared components, page templates, content hierarchy and internal links all contribute to how the site behaves as a whole.

We aim for a structure where repeated elements are consistent and individual pages can still have the space they need to communicate their subject properly.

This becomes particularly useful as a website grows and new service pages, projects or supporting content are introduced.

RESPONSIVE DEVELOPMENT

One website. Different screens. No afterthought

Responsive development is not simply making a desktop layout smaller. Navigation, spacing, typography, images, buttons and content blocks often need to behave differently as the available space changes.

We build with those changes in mind so the website remains readable and usable rather than becoming a compressed version of the desktop experience.

MAINTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

Launch day is not the end of the website

A business website often changes after launch. Services evolve, pages are added, information is updated and the business itself moves forward.

That makes maintainability important. Repeated structures should remain consistent, page organisation should be understandable and future changes should not require rebuilding unrelated parts of the site.

We therefore consider the practical life of the website, not only how it looks on the day it goes live.
