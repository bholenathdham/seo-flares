02 · Technical SEO

Make the site easier to understand from the inside out.

Technical SEO is about the conditions around your pages. We look at the parts that help search engines discover, interpret and connect the important areas of a website.

  • ✓ Crawlability & indexing
    Reviewing access, indexation signals and important technical barriers.
  • ✓ Site architecture
    Creating clearer relationships between pages, sections and internal links.
  • ✓ Redirects & canonical signals
    Helping old, duplicate or changed URLs resolve in a deliberate way.
  • ✓ Performance foundations
    Considering loading behaviour, stable layouts and other technical quality signals.

08 · FAQ

A few useful answers before we start.

What areas of SEO can you help with?

We can work across technical SEO, on-page optimization, content structure, search intent, internal linking, local search foundations and ongoing refinement, depending on the project.

Can SEO be added to an existing website?

Yes. We can review the existing site and identify practical areas for technical, structural, content and on-page improvement.

Can SEO be considered while building a new website?

Yes. Website architecture, page hierarchy, content structure and technical foundations can be considered during the build rather than retrofitted later.

Do you provide keyword research?

Search intent and keyword research can be part of the SEO approach when it is relevant to the project and the pages being planned or improved.

Do you work with local businesses?

Yes. Local search considerations can be included for businesses whose customers search by location, service area or local relevance.

How do we start?
