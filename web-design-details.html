What goes into a well designed website?

Website design is more than choosing colours, fonts and images. We look at how your business should be presented, how people will move through the site and how every important piece of information can work together.

01 The idea

Your website should make your business easier to understand

A visitor should not have to work out what you do, where to go or what to do next. Our job is to turn your business, services and content into a digital experience that feels clear and considered.

That starts before the visual design. We look at the purpose of the website, the people it needs to speak to, the information they need and the actions that matter. From there, we shape the structure and visual direction around those requirements.

Good design is not decoration. It is the way information, interface and visual identity work together.
02 What we do

From the page structure to the final details

The exact scope depends on the project, but these are the areas we can work through when building or redesigning a website.

01

Website structure & page planning

We organize the pages, navigation and content hierarchy so visitors can understand the website and move through it naturally.

  • Page and navigation planning
  • Content hierarchy
  • Clear page relationships
02

Visual direction

We shape the visual language around your brand rather than applying a generic look to every project.

  • Typography and spacing
  • Colour and visual accents
  • Images, sections and interface patterns
03

UX / UI design

We consider how people interact with the website, where their attention goes and how important actions are presented.

  • Navigation and user journeys
  • Readable interfaces
  • Calls to action and interaction states
04

Responsive implementation

The experience is adapted for desktop, tablet and mobile rather than simply shrinking the desktop version.

  • Flexible layouts
  • Mobile navigation and controls
  • Responsive typography and spacing
05

Front-end development

We translate the approved design into the working website, including reusable components and required front-end behaviour.

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript implementation
  • Reusable sections and components
  • Forms and required integrations
06

Refinement & launch preparation

Before launch, we review the finished experience across devices and refine the details that affect usability and presentation.

  • Responsive review
  • Content and link checks
  • Final visual polish

04 Responsive design

Designed for the way people actually browse

A website can look excellent on a large monitor and still become frustrating on a phone. We consider responsive behaviour throughout the design and development process.

✓ Layouts adapt naturally ✓ Content remains readable ✓ Navigation stays practical ✓ Buttons and controls remain usable

A business website, online store and focused landing page do not need the same structure. We adapt the work to the purpose.

Business

Business websites

Commerce

E-commerce

Content

CMS websites

08 Questions

Before we start

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can review the existing structure and content, keep what is useful and redesign the experience around the current goals and requirements.

Will the new website be responsive?

Yes. Responsive behaviour is considered during design and development so the website remains practical across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Can you design an e-commerce website?

Yes. E-commerce work can include category structure, product presentation, shopping journeys and responsive interfaces, depending on the project.

Can you work with a CMS?

Yes. Where a CMS is appropriate, the website can be structured around manageable content and clear page organization.

Can SEO foundations be considered during the website project?

Yes. We can consider structure, hierarchy, responsive implementation and other SEO-ready foundations during the build. Ongoing SEO can be handled separately.

What should I send when enquiring?

Tell us what you are building or redesigning, what you want the website to achieve and anything that is currently causing problems. Existing websites, references and content are useful but not required to start the conversation.
